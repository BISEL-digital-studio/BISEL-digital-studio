### 📱 Swipoff · iOS screen-time app
[swipoff.com](https://swipoff.com)

A side project built in my free time in October 2026. You earn screen minutes by walking, reading or going to bed on time. When they run out, a friend you chose holds your switch and decides whether to turn your apps back on. The friend installs nothing; they just open a link.

- Apple Family Controls (Distribution) entitlement approved; running on my iPhone, App Store submission pending
- Keyholder web page on Cloudflare Pages, Supabase backend planned (EU)

### 💊 FarmaShift · pharmacy staffing marketplace (in progress)

A two-sided marketplace for pharmacy staffing in Spain. Pharmacies post shifts and jobs, and pharmacists, students and technicians find and apply for them. Pharmacies struggle to cover sick leave and holidays at short notice, and students need flexible experience. No platform in Spain was built for this.

- Pharmacies post single shifts, part-time, permanent or internship roles, filter candidates by level, distance, availability and rating, and accept or reject with one click
- Professionals search jobs on a map colour-coded by urgency, track each application from pending to completed, and build a profile with work history, badges and ranks based on hours worked
- Real-time chat that unlocks on acceptance, a shift calendar, and pharmacy profiles synced from Google Maps
- React, TypeScript, Vite, Tailwind · Supabase (Postgres, Realtime, row-level security on every table) · Google Maps and Places APIs
- In active development, launching with pharmacies in Catalonia first
